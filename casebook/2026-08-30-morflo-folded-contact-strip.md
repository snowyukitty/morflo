## Summary

A local media converter represented by one folded contact strip. The
contact-strip concept won over a swatch book, buckle, and folding ruler because
it preserves continuity while making transition visible without arrows.

## What failed first

The first contact strip used two dark green masses with no edge treatment. It
held on white, but its upper frame almost vanished on the dark review row,
leaving color at 3. A 32-unit warm structural outline restored the complete
object on dark and mid-grey without filling either negative-space window. The
solid-window variant also proved that the windows, not merely the zig-zag
footprint, make the object read as a contact strip; the final pass retained
openings large enough to remain two pixels tall at 16 px.

## Lessons

- [ ] A light structural outline can preserve a dark product palette across
      light and dark system surfaces without changing the object silhouette.
- [ ] Two media windows need at least two output pixels of negative space at
      16 px; larger holes carried the noun more reliably than perforations.
